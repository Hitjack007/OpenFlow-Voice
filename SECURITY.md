# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Mark Greene and the OpenFlow Voice community take security bugs seriously. We appreciate your efforts to responsibly disclose your findings, and will make every effort to acknowledge your contributions.

OpenFlow Voice handles microphone audio and injects text into other applications via Accessibility APIs. Any vulnerability that could cause unauthorized audio capture, unintended text injection, or exposure of transcribed content should be treated as high priority.

To report a security issue, please use the GitHub Security Advisory ["Report a Vulnerability"](../../security/advisories/new) tab rather than opening a public issue.

A response will be sent indicating the next steps in handling your report. After the initial reply, you will be kept informed of the progress towards a fix and full announcement, and may be asked for additional information or guidance.

Report security bugs in third-party dependencies (e.g., FluidAudio) to the maintainers of that package directly.
